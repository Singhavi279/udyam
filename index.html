Why Uttar Pradesh, why now

Scale है। अब coordination चाहिए।

Uttar Pradesh sits at the centre of India's MSME engine: over 90 lakh enterprises driving employment, exports, and district-level industrialisation through ODOP, digital single-window clearances, and targeted credit expansion.

Achieving a $1 trillion economy by 2030 demands sharper collaboration between government, MSMEs, investors, and solution providers. This summit creates that focused UP platform, bringing decision-makers and enablers to one room to close execution gaps, accelerate innovation, and convert UP's MSME base into a globally competitive ecosystem.
